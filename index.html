Ofform is Displaay’s 7×5 grid face: uppercase and lowercase share one height, so every piece sits the same way on its square. The sentence is set in Serrif Light and wraps to the board’s width. In board layout, small text is any item in brackets, like (live). Sizes are in points on the 900-wide poster and scale with the format.

Details

How the layout is built

The game is replayed move by move. The pieces left on the board after the last move give the title its squares: characters fill them in reading order, a8 to h1. The info is set as one sentence below the board, or, in board layout, on the same squares of a smaller board. Each background square is tinted by how many of the game’s positions it was occupied in, from the start position to the end. The destination of the last move is filled in the tint, or set in the accent colour.
