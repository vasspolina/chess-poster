# Chess poster

Poster for chess.irl №4, 12 May 2026, Greenpoint, Brooklyn.

The poster sets one character of the title, in capitals, on each piece left on the board at the end of a Kasparov win whose final position has exactly as many pieces as the title has characters; punctuation rides with the letter before it. For CHESS.IRL №4, ten units, that is Kasparov–Kasimdzhanov, Batumi 2001, after 70.Ra8. The generator carries all 2,128 of his games from PGN Mentor (`games.json`, 1976 to 2021) in a dropdown, and switches to the longest fitting win whenever the title's length has no position in the chosen game. Spare piece squares take the date. The squares behind the type are tinted by how often each square was occupied during the game, in one tint or stepping through the Displaay palette (brown, amber, orange, blue) by use; hairline outlines are a switch, off by default. The info lines use the same squares on a smaller board at the bottom.

## Files

- `index.html` — Position Poster, the generator. Dark only, set after displaay.net: black ground, regular weights, hairline rules, the Displaay swatch palette (blue #222FF4, brown #A06F46, amber #E79A3A, orange #E27631). The poster title is set in Ofform (Displaay), embedded from `fonts/` so exports carry it; the tool and the title are set in Panell (Displaay, seven widths); the date and info are a few lines of PP Mori Book laid over the bottom row of the board, like a list over big type, with the line breaks as typed and the title in the accent colour. The title picks the position in the game with as many pieces as it has characters (11 to 32 across the two Kasparov games). Formats: Instagram post 1080 × 1350, Instagram story 1080 × 1920, RA poster at the A-series ratio RA asks for, 1000 × 1414 before export scale. Ground swatches set the whole scheme on black, blue, brown, amber or orange; the canvas has the same `ground` option. Paste any game as PGN, type a title and info lines, pick type, pattern, colours and format, then export SVG or PNG. Replays the game with chess.js from cdnjs. Published as a Claude artifact: https://claude.ai/artifact/AzL2CbgQvpEJKtUChEy53i
- `fonts/` — Displaay trial files (Ofform, Serrif Light, Panell Regular in seven widths) and PP Mori Book. Trial fonts are for testing only. Trial licence: for testing only; swap for a licensed copy before printing.
- `games.json` — Kasparov's games from PGN Mentor, one record each: players, event, site, date, result, moves, capture count, ply count.
- `design/project/` — the hand-set poster as a Claude Design canvas: `Main.dc.html` (900 × 1200), `Mobile.dc.html` (1080 × 1920 story) and `canvas.json`, the canvas index. Canvas: https://claude.ai/artifact/SePfEwm6FGjnZUh2jFiZ6N

The `.dc.html` files are Design Component pages and need the canvas runtime (`support.js`) to render; they are kept here as source.
